Ancient Kingdoms

Heritage & Historical Sites

From remote rock fortresses to sacred cities and medieval capitals — eight UNESCO World Heritage treasures await within the Cultural Triangle.

UNESCO · 5th C.

Sigiriya — The Lion Rock

A fifth-century fortress-palace rising 200 metres above the jungle, guarded in legend by a colossal lion and adorned with the "Heavenly Maidens" frescoes.

Sacred City

Temple of the Sacred Tooth

Kandy's golden-roofed shrine houses the sacred relic of the Buddha's tooth, celebrated each year in the dazzling Esala Perahera.

First Capital · 437 BC

Anuradhapura

The cradle of Sinhalese civilisation, where the sacred Bo tree — grown from a cutting of the original Bodhi — has been worshipped for over 2,200 years.

Medieval · 12th C.

Polonnaruwa

The grand capital of the 12th century, home to the reclining Gal Vihara Buddhas carved from a single granite wall and Parakramabahu's great lake.

Hill Capital

Kandy

The last capital of the Sinhala kings, cradled among misty hills and famed for its lake, temples and the Royal Botanical Gardens.

Coastal Fort · 17th C.

Galle Fort

A colonial walled city of Dutch ramparts, cobbled lanes and ocean views standing watch over the southern coast.

Sacred Peak

Dambulla Cave Temple

Ochre-hued sacred caves filled with golden Buddhas and vivid 1,800-square-metres of ancient murals.

Highlands

Central Highlands

The cloud forests of the Knuckles Range and the Horton Plains — the island's last wild highland wilderness.

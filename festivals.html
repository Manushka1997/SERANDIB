Celebrations Around the Year

Festivals & Observances

Esala Perahera

Kandy's grandest procession — a glowing river of dancers, drummers, fire-spinners and elephants honouring the Sacred Tooth.

Vesak & Full Moon Poya

Every full moon is a sacred day; Vesak crowns the year with lanterns, alms-giving and sil lights.

Avurudu (New Year)

The Sinhala and Tamil New Year — traditional games, milk-rice and auspicious rituals.

Deepavali

The Tamil festival of lights — oil lamps and glowing colour in the north and across the island.

An Island Steeped in Legend

Known to ancient mariners as Serendib, the sailors' land of gold and spices, Sri Lanka shines as a tapestry of civilizations that have thrived on this teardrop island for over two millennia.

Walk among the ruins of lost capitals, climb the towering dagobas that pierce the sky, and trace the footsteps of kings, monks and pilgrims who shaped a culture where tradition and history live side by side.
